Where alerts go a watch with no URL only writes to the log

Any endpoint accepting a JSON POST works — the body carries a ready-made text field alongside the structured fields, which is what Slack, Discord (add /slack to the webhook URL), Mattermost and Home Assistant all expect. On Unraid the usual targets are a Gotify or ntfy container on the same box.

Alerts are only as timely as your crawls: they are evaluated after each one, so the full_crawl schedule in config.yaml sets how quickly a drop can reach you.
